### My take:

The article above covers the main caching scenarios, which will be enough for
99% of tasks. For the remaining 1%, you can check the description of all
`Cache-Control` header properties
([link](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Headers/Cache-Control))
or google your specific case.

P.S. If your project uses NestJS, you can play around with `CacheInterceptor`,
like
[here](https://blog.shahid.codes/how-to-exclude-a-route-from-caching-in-nestjs)
