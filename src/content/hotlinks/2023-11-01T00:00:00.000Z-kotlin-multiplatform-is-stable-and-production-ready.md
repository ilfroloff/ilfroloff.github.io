The family of cross-mobile development solutions just grew:
[JetBrains](https://jetbrains.com/) has released a production-ready version of
its framework for simultaneous Android/iOS/Web/TV development in Kotlin. A
strong competitor to the [React Native](https://reactnative.dev/)/[Flutter](https://flutter.dev/)
that web developers are used to, but with much closer access to the mobile
APIs, especially on Android.
