The cross-mobile development solutions family has grown -
[JetBrains](https://jetbrains.com/) has released a production-ready version of
their framework for simultaneous development on Android/iOS/Web/TV in Kotlin. A
strong competitor to the familiar-to-web-developers
[React Native](https://reactnative.dev/)/[Flutter](https://flutter.dev/), but
with much closer access to the mobile API, especially on Android platforms
